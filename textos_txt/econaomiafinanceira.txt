BCG Matrix => business intelligence tool that helps organizations assess their product portfoli's performance and potential
-there are four different categories - stars, cash cows, question marks and dogs

stars-> these are products with high market shares that are in fast-growing markets. They have the potential to generate sognificant profits due to their position in this industry. So if a company dominates 90% of the market share for a nich that is growing very fast the product that they're selling is likely to be star.

cash cows > are products that have hight market shares in markets that aren't growing so fast. while they may not have as much growth potential, they generate steady cash flow, witch is often desirable for businesses.
so similar to stars, but they don't have the future potentia and insead they're making money right now

Question marks -> are prodcts that companies may have low market share in, however the market is likely to expand in the future.
They require consideration as to whether a company should invest in it to develop it's market share and potentially create it into a star or to left it go and become a dog

Dogs -> are products that have a low market share and a market grotw in a market that isn't growing
they won't generate significant profits for the company and if a company neds to reduce some of its services that its offering, the dogs are the first to go.

The bcg matrix can help a company allocate its resources effectively by suggesting strategies for products or services that fall into each specific quadrant.

Unemployment
So unemployment is spli into three categories, cyclical, structural, and frictional

Cyclical unemployment results from fluctuations in the overall economy. When the economy enters a recession or a downturn, demand for goods and service decreases, causing businesses to scale back production. As a result, they lay off workers leading to ciclical unemployment.
Consider the construction industry During an economcc boo, there's a high demand for new homes and infrastructural projects. Construction companies hire more workers to meet this growing demand. However, when a recession hits, the demand for new projects drops, causing these companies to cut back on their workforce, rsulting in cyclical unemployment for construction workers.
Similarly, in the tourism sector, when the economy is thriving, people have more disposable income to travel and explore. This leads to inreased demand for flights, hotels, enertainment like movies, and so on.
Yest, in and economica downturn, people tighten theri budgets, leading to reduced travel and low demand for these tourism-related services. And as a result, there are losses in jobs inthis industry.
In both examples, cyclical unemployment is closely tied to the fluctuations in the business cycle which we saw earlier.
As the economy cantracts and expands, industries that heavily  rely on consumer spending and investments can experience shifts in employment levels due to the changes in demand.

Whats is monetary policy?

Economic stability and grohth   -> unstable economy -> central bank(injects monetary policy(controlling the money suply)) -> economic stability and growth

Monetry policy involves a bank's managemnt of a country's money supply and interest rates to influence economic activity. By adjusting interst rates and employing tools like opn market operations, central banks can control inflation, encourage borrowing, and spending during slow economic periods using expansionary monetary policy, or cool down an overheating economy using a contractionary monetary policy.
Expansionary monetary policy is when central bnks reduce interest rates to make borrowing money easier for consumers. This is because the interest they would havee to pay on a loan rdues, taking the loan much more appealing.
Contractionary monetry policy, on the other hand, is wen central bnks increase interest rates to make borrowing more epensive, causing borrowers to pay more interest on their loans, deceasing the appeal of these loans.

An intereting example of monetay policy in action is japan's use of interest rates to stimulate the economy. Typically, in a country that is using expansionary mnetary policy, interest rates could be lowered for 3% to 2%, for example, to encourage borrowing. japan, however, decided to take an unconventional approach. The bank of japan set an interest rate of negative -0.1. This is extremely unique and a chice made by japan's governament to meet their specific needs. This was their attempt to overcome deflation, which was a big issue in japan. By making interest rates negative, it makes t more economical to borrow and spend money than just holding on to it. As a result, people kept taking on loans because it was generating that money, and as a result, they started spending more, which stimulated japan's economy.
